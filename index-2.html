Questions

Do I need technical skills to join as a humanities major? +

No. Zero coding is required. Your value is the context, critical thinking, and communication you bring to product decisions.

What does the beta include? +

Beta testers get early access to the platform and a direct line to shape how matching, fellowships, and collaboration work.

I run a startup. Why should I care? +

Products built without sociological context tend to miss real users. Lumen gives you affordable, diverse perspective before you scale.

What happens to my information? +

We only use your name and email to contact you about beta access. We won’t sell your data.

How does matching work? +

Nobody should pay for the mistake of a bad hire. Every match is vetted personally, followed by a trial period and then an equitable temporary contract. Ratings from both sides are confidential, and we check in if something isn’t working.

Is Lumen only for the United States? +

We are starting small, but the goal is to be open to everyone across borders, so you can pursue technology from wherever home is.
